Help and terms

Delivery

We ship from our warehouse in Gdansk in 1-2 working days.

  • Standard delivery: €4.90, 2-4 working days. Free for orders from €150.
  • Express delivery: €12.90, next working day for orders before 13:00.

Returns

You can return unworn shoes in the original box within 30 days from the delivery. Write to us through the contact form with your order number. We send a free return label. We pay back the money within 14 days after we receive the shoes.

About us

Pace & Sole is a small shoe shop from Gdansk. We sell shoes for running, hiking and every day. This shop is a demo for a university study: it ships no shoes and takes no money.

  2. The prices include VAT. Delivery costs are shown at the checkout.
  3. The contract starts when we confirm the order by email.
  4. You can pay on delivery or by bank transfer.
  5. You can withdraw from the contract within 30 days without a reason.
  6. We repair or replace faulty products within 2 years.

Privacy

We use the data of an order only to deliver it. This demo shop records how visitors use the pages (clicks, scroll, key timing) for a university study. It does not record the text that you type, your name or your IP address.
